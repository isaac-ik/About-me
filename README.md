## Featured work

### Dry Bean quality classification → automated sorting conveyor
[`DryBean-Quality-ML-Classification`](https://github.com/isaac-ik/DryBean-Quality-ML-Classification)

A classifier that identifies 7 bean varieties from 16 shape features (13,611 samples, PCA + model comparison), extended into a **3D-printable, camera-guided sorting machine**: OpenCV measures each bean on the belt, the model predicts its variety and an Arduino opens the right servo gate. Includes parametric CAD, printable parts, firmware and a Blender gate-timing simulation.
*Value: automated quality inspection for food packaging.*

### Six Sigma DMAIC: defect reduction in electrical-box manufacturing
[`DMAIC-Defect-Reduction-`](https://github.com/isaac-ik/DMAIC-Defect-Reduction-)

Three years of real non-conformity data from a manufacturer (under NDA). X-bar charts tracked the process, and a two-way ANOVA showed that **operator proficiency (p = 0.013) and machine type (p = 0.03)** drive defect rates. That pointed training and resources at the real causes, and the control phase locked the gains in with SPC, a control plan and updated SOPs.
*Value: lower scrap and rework, focused training spend.*

### Explainable building energy prediction
[`Energy-Consumption-Analysis-and-Modelling`](https://github.com/isaac-ik/Energy-Consumption-Analysis-and-Modelling)
